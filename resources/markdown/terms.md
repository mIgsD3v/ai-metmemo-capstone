# Terms of Service

*Last updated: October 31, 2026*

MetMemo is a digital business card with an AI assistant, built as a capstone project and offered as is. By creating
an account you agree to these terms.

## Your account

Keep your password safe; you're responsible for what happens in your account. You can sign up with an email and
password or with Google.

## Your content

The cards, contacts and notes you add are yours. Only add details about other people that they shared with you, such
as the business card they handed you, and use them to follow up with them, not for spam or bulk messaging.

## The AI assistant

The assistant suggests answers and drafts messages from your own contacts and notes. It can make mistakes: check
anything it writes before you rely on it or send it. It never sends messages or changes your contacts by itself.

## Acceptable use

Don't use MetMemo to break the law, harass anyone, send unsolicited bulk messages, or try to access other people's
data or disrupt the service.

## Availability

MetMemo is a student project. It may change, be unavailable at times, or be shut down, and it comes without
warranties of any kind.
