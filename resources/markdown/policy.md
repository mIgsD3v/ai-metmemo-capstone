# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 31, 2026*

MetMemo is a digital business card with an AI assistant that helps you remember the people you meet and follow up
with them. It was built as a capstone project. This page explains what MetMemo stores, why, and who else handles it.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash) and, if you add one, a
  profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's name, email address and a Google
  account id, and nothing else: no access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or calendar.
- **Your cards:** the details you choose to put on your digital business cards.
- **Your contacts and notes:** the people you save (typed in, scanned from a business card photo, or left through
  your public card's form) and the notes you write about your meetings.
- **People who visit your public card:** if a visitor fills in the "Send your details" form, their name, contact
  details and message are saved as a contact for the card's owner only.
- **Assistant chats:** the questions you ask the assistant and its answers, so you can reopen a chat later.

## How we use it

Only to run MetMemo for you: showing your cards and contacts, reminding you who to follow up with, reading business
cards you scan, and answering your questions about your own contacts and notes. We don't sell your data, show ads,
or share it with other users. Each account only ever sees its own contacts and notes.

## Services that process data for us

- **OpenAI** reads business card photos you scan, turns notes into search vectors, and writes the assistant's
  answers and drafts. Phone numbers and email addresses are masked before notes are sent to the assistant.
- **Langfuse** records how the assistant answered (for quality checks and debugging), with phone numbers and email
  addresses masked.
- **DigitalOcean** hosts the server and its database in Singapore.
- **Google** handles "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you use it.

## Keeping it safe

All traffic uses HTTPS. The database and the AI service are not reachable from the internet. You can turn on
two-factor authentication in your profile.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete any card, contact or note, delete assistant chats, and hide a card so its link and QR code
stop working. To delete your account and its data, contact us at the address below.
